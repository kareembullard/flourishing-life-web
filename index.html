🌱 Personal Growth Tracker

A 14-step positive-psychology journey with habit tracking, PERMA well-being scoring, and daily practice logging.

This is a portfolio demo. All data (journey steps, habits, assessments, exercises, community content) is fictional sample data, seeded on first load. Everything is editable — mark steps complete, log habits, retake the PERMA assessment, add goals and exercises. Edits save to your browser only (localStorage); nothing is ever sent to a server. Use Reset in the toolbar to restore the original demo data.
